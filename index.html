How to play

  1. Drag across adjacent letters.
  2. Move in any direction, including diagonals, and turn at any tile.
  3. You cannot reuse a tile in the same word.
  4. Words need 3 or more letters. Tiles marked ×2 or ×3 boost a word's base score.
  5. Find as many words as you can before time runs out.

Example: drag T → A → N and release to submit TAN.
